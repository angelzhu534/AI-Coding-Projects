Contract types — identify before routing
⚡
Power Purchase Agreement
PPA · Core commercial contract
What it is
Long-term commitment to deliver power to a utility or corporate buyer. 15–20 year term with performance milestones, capacity guarantees, and liquidated damages.
Key obligations to track
Commercial Operation Date (COD), capacity guarantees, availability requirements, augmentation triggers, renewal windows
Risk level
🔴 High — missed COD = liquidated damages
🌾
Land Lease / Easement
Real estate · Project site
What it is
Long-term lease of hundreds of acres from private landowners for each solar project site. Often 25–40 year terms with renewal options.
Key obligations to track
Renewal option windows, rent escalation schedules, amendment history, construction commencement dates, access rights
Risk level
🔴 High — missed renewal = loss of project site
🔌
Interconnection Agreement
CAISO / ERCOT · Grid connection
What it is
Governs how IPX connects its solar/battery projects to the CAISO or ERCOT grid. Defines cost-sharing for grid upgrades and technical requirements.
Key obligations to track
Queue position milestones, study completion dates, cost allocation deadlines, execution windows
Risk level
🔴 High — missed milestone = lost queue position
🏗️
EPC Contract
Engineering · Procurement · Construction
What it is
Contract with contractors who build solar and battery storage projects. Defines scope, schedule, price, and performance guarantees.
Key obligations to track
COD milestone, liquidated damages provisions, warranty periods, change order log, performance testing schedule
Risk level
🟡 Medium-High — delay claims and cost overruns
🏦
Project Finance Agreement
Debt · Tax equity · Lender obligations
What it is
Complex debt and equity financing for each project — often hundreds of millions. Governed by loan/credit agreements with lenders and institutional investors.
Key obligations to track
Quarterly lender reporting, covenant compliance, draw conditions, insurance requirements, maturity dates
Risk level
🔴 High — covenant breach = technical default
⚖️
Outside Counsel / Vendor
Law firms · Consultants · Service providers
What it is
Engagement letters with law firms and vendor agreements with consultants and service providers. Governs scope, rates, billing guidelines, and conflict waivers.
Key obligations to track
Billing guideline compliance, budget vs actuals per matter, rate agreement renewals, invoice review cadence
Risk level
🟢 Medium — spend overruns, guideline violations

🔍 Attorney Review & NegotiationAttorney-owned
Attorney reviews and redlines — legal ops tracks status in Evisort and follows up if stalled
Coordinate scheduling of negotiation calls between parties if needed
Maintain version control in Evisort — tag each redline version with date and party
Flag to requesting business team when review is taking longer than the agreed SLA

Legal ops does not participate in substantive legal negotiation. Our role here is tracking status, maintaining version control, and making sure nothing stalls without visibility.
4
✅ Internal Approval & Signing Authority CheckEvisort workflow
Confirm contract value and identify required signatories per the signing authority matrix above
Route for internal approval in Evisort — notify required approvers via Slack with deadline
Follow up on pending approvals after 48 hours — escalate to Janelle if unresponsive beyond 5 days
Confirm Finance has approved any budget implications before final sign-off
Once all internal approvals are collected, move to DocuSign execution
5
✍️ Execution via DocuSignDocuSign
Create DocuSign envelope with the final approved version of the agreement
Route in correct signing order — internal signatories first, then counterparty
Confirm counterparty signatory name, title, and email match the agreement
Monitor DocuSign queue daily — follow up with any signatory pending more than 48 hours
Download and store fully executed agreement immediately upon completion
Do not allow work to begin while DocuSign is in progress. A pending signature is not an executed contract. This is the most common source of unauthorized work starting before execution.
6
🗂️ Storage, Tagging & Obligation EntryEvisort
Upload executed agreement to Evisort — tag with contract type, counterparty, project, and value
Enter all key dates — effective date, expiration, renewal windows, COD, milestones
Set automated alerts in Evisort at 180/90/60/30 days before all key dates
Notify the relevant business team that the contract is executed and provide key dates
For project finance agreements — flag quarterly lender reporting obligations in Finance's calendar
Save a copy to Relativity if the agreement may be relevant to any active or anticipated litigation
This step is where most contract management breaks down. An unsigned agreement stored without obligation dates entered is just a file — not a managed contract. Every executed agreement must have all key dates in Evisort before this step is complete.

Evisort — how we use it
Evisort is our CLM platform — the single source of truth for all IPX agreements. Every executed contract must be in Evisort with proper tagging and obligation dates entered. If it's not in Evisort, it doesn't exist from a legal ops perspective.
📥 Intake & Routing
All contract requests route through Evisort. Classify by type, assign to attorney, track status. Business teams should not go directly to attorneys.
📄 Storage & Tagging
Every executed agreement stored with consistent tagging — contract type, counterparty, project, value, effective date, expiration, key milestones.
⏰ Obligation Tracking
Automated alerts at 180/90/60/30 days before every key date. Renewal windows, COD dates, lender reporting, milestones — all tracked here.
📊 Reporting & KPIs
Weekly contract status reports to business teams. Quarterly KPI reports to Jen and CLO — matter volume, spend, turnaround times, expiring agreements.

No contract should surprise us at renewal. Every agreement has a renewal window tracked and alerts firing well in advance so attorneys have time to engage.

⚡ What IPX Power does

IPX Power develops, owns, and operates utility-scale solar and battery storage projects. The legal ops team supports a complex contract portfolio spanning decades-long PPAs, hundreds of land leases, EPC construction contracts, and project finance agreements worth hundreds of millions of dollars.

Every project has a lifecycle: development → permitting → financing → construction → commercial operation → long-term O&M. Legal ops touches every stage through contracts.

Missing a deadline in this business has real consequences — a missed land lease renewal window can mean losing a project site. A missed PPA COD triggers liquidated damages. Take every deadline seriously.

Industry glossary — terms you'll encounter constantly
COD
Commercial Operation Date — when a solar/battery project starts delivering power commercially. Missing this in a PPA triggers liquidated damages.
PPA
Power Purchase Agreement — long-term contract committing IPX to deliver power to a buyer (utility or corporate).
CAISO
California ISO — the grid operator for most of California. IPX projects in CA interconnect through CAISO's queue process.
ERCOT
Electric Reliability Council of Texas — the Texas grid operator. Projects in TX interconnect through ERCOT.
EPC
Engineering, Procurement & Construction — the contract type for companies that build IPX's projects. EPC contractors are responsible for construction timelines and performance.
Tax equity
A form of project finance where investors provide capital in exchange for federal tax credits (ITC/PTC) generated by the solar project.
FERC
Federal Energy Regulatory Commission — federal regulator for wholesale electricity markets. IPX must maintain market-based rate authorization for its projects.
Liquidated damages (LD)
Pre-agreed damages in a contract for specific failures (e.g. missing COD). Critical to track because they can be large and automatic.
CLM
Contract Lifecycle Management — the software category Evisort belongs to. Covers intake through obligation tracking.
FEOC
Foreign Entity of Concern — IRA-related compliance requirement affecting supply chain documentation for projects claiming ITC/PTC credits.
Covenant
A binding obligation in a project finance agreement — e.g. maintaining certain financial ratios. Breach = technical default.
ITC / PTC
Investment Tax Credit / Production Tax Credit — federal tax credits under the IRA that make solar and battery projects financially viable. Drive project finance structure.
