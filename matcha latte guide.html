Iced Matcha Latte
a step-by-step guide
Ingredients (1 serving)
2 tsp ceremonial matcha
2 tbsp hot water (70–80°C)
¾ cup oat or dairy milk
1–2 tsp sweetener of choice
a handful of ice cubes
Steps
Step 1
🧂
Sift matchainto your bowl to remove clumps
Step 2
💧
Add 2 tbsp waterat 70–80°C — never boiling
Step 3
🎋
Whisk in W motionuntil smooth and frothy paste
Step 4
🍯
Stir in sweetenerwhile matcha is still warm
Step 5
🧊
Fill glass with icethen pour cold milk over it
Step 6
🍵
Pour matcha on topgently for beautiful layers
Glass assembly — layer order
1
Cold milk
fills about ¾ of the glass
2
Ice cubes
a generous handful on top
3
Matcha paste
pour slowly — creates the gradient!
Sweetness & syrups
🍯 How much to add
Subtle: ½ tsp — matcha forward
Balanced: 1 tsp — most people's sweet spot
Sweet: 2 tsp — café style
✨ Syrup ideas
Vanilla — classic, crowd-pleaser
Honey — earthy, pairs perfectly
Brown sugar — caramel depth
Lavender — floral & dreamy
Coconut — tropical twist
Pro tip: Mix sweetener into the warm matcha paste before adding ice — it dissolves much better than stirring into cold milk!
