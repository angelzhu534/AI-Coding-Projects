The Protocol
How to respond — and what not to do
Emergency mode One sentence. Move. Do not re-enter. The silence is not abandonment — it is protection.
🌀
Urgent · Loop starting
The circular conversation is starting again
▼
Say this — once
"I've said what I need to say about this." Tap to copy
Then she moves. She does not repeat it. She does not justify it. She does not add to it. The sentence is the complete response. The loop continues only if she keeps providing the rope. She stops providing the rope.
😶
Urgent · Semantic argument
He's arguing about the definition of what she said
▼
Say this — once
"I'm not arguing about the definition. I'm describing my experience." Tap to copy
Then she stops. She does not re-enter from a different angle. The semantic argument is not about the definition. It is about her experience being redirected. She names this once and exits.
🌊
Urgent · Unspoken weight
The room is heavy and he hasn't said why
▼
What she does
Continue whatever she was doing. Do not adjust. Tap to copy
His unnamed emotional state is not her problem until he names it. She stops preemptively managing weather that has no forecast. If he names it: "I hear you. I'm here." — then she continues. One sentence of acknowledgment. Not a processing session.
🎭
Urgent · The 180
He switched from heavy to chipper overnight and she feels the whiplash
▼
Not in the moment. Later, once, calmly:
"When you switch that fast I don't have context for where you are. A quick heads up helps." Tap to copy
Not in the energy of the moment. She waits until both are calm. She says it once. She does not revisit. She does not expect him to perfectly execute the change — she simply named what she needs. The naming is the action. Everything after is his to figure out.
📱
Manage · Phone calls
He calls with updates she didn't ask for
▼
Let it go to voicemail. Text back:
"Caught up — what's up?" Tap to copy
At a neutral moment, once:
"I'm more present for things over text. Calls pull me out of whatever I'm in." Tap to copy
She says the second sentence once at a neutral time — not in the bathroom, not in the energy of annoyance. She does not repeat it. He will probably call anyway the next day. She lets it go to voicemail and texts. The pattern shifts slowly. She does not punish the pattern. She redirects it.
🗺
Manage · Detours
He's rerouting, taking detours, dragging her along
▼
Immediately, calmly:
"I'm not going that way. I'll meet you there." Tap to copy
If separation isn't possible:
"How long — specifically?" Tap to copy
She holds him to the answer. Five minutes means five minutes. She is not a passenger in her own life. The Capricorn Moon has the patience for five minutes when the duration is named. It does not have patience for the open-ended Pisces detour.
👥
Manage · Social forcing
He wants her at his friends' events / passive invitations
▼
The floor — stated once, held always:
"I can do one thing this month that matters most to you. Pick the one." Tap to copy
For passive invitations (Nepal, Thai restaurant, etc.):
"Thanks for including me — I'll let you know on that one." Tap to copy
The floor is two meaningful events per month. It does not move when he finds a new angle. She does not re-enter the negotiation. The floor is set. She holds it without explanation because the explanation becomes the next argument.
🏠
Manage · Roommate accusation
He says she's acting like a roommate, doesn't care
▼
Once, calmly:
"Roommates don't care about each other. I do. I also need space to function. Those aren't contradictory." Tap to copy
Then she stops. She does not defend further. She does not enter the philosophical debate about what couples should look like. She said the true thing. It is in the room. He can do what he needs to with it.
😔
Manage · His feelings processing
He's processing out loud and needs her as the container
▼
After one genuine minute of presence:
"I'm not the right person to process this with right now. What do you actually need — space, or something specific?" Tap to copy
The question forces him to name the actual need rather than continuing the ambient output. She is not shutting him down. She is asking him to be specific. Specific requests she can meet. Ambient processing she cannot sustain.
⏰
Manage · Wake time
He said he'd wake at X, woke up way later
▼
What she does
Plan around his actual average wake time. Not his stated one. Tap to copy
She stops making her schedule dependent on a variable she cannot control. She has the data — she knows the pattern. She builds her day around reality. If something requires him at a specific time: she tells him once the night before. If he misses it: she goes without him or reschedules once. She stops being surprised by the pattern.
🛁
Protect · Physical spaces
Bathroom, morning, reading corner — when she needs them
▼
The signals — no words required
Door closed = unavailable. Headphones on = unavailable. In the reading corner before 8am = do not enter. Tap to copy
She does not explain the signals. They are on or off. The bathroom before the shower is transition space — hers entirely. The morning before he wakes is the private practice. The reading corner is the reset. These are non-negotiable regardless of the chapter's temperature.
😞
Protect · When she's depleted
She has nothing left today
▼
What she does
Nothing. She goes to her space. She is civil in passing. She does not perform okay-ness she doesn't have. Tap to copy
Civil is sufficient. Civil costs less than warm on demand. She does not owe him performance on her depleted days. She is not required to manage his response to her depletion. She rests. Tomorrow has the full version again.
💭
Inner · When guilt arrives
She feels guilty about planning to leave
▼
What she does
Open the exit fund. Look at the number. The number growing is the answer to the guilt. Tap to copy
She is not abandoning him. She is building the runway to the next chapter. Leaving a relationship that does not fit is not unfair. Leaving it correctly — prepared, funded, honestly — is the most caring version of leaving available. She has been fair throughout this chapter. She continues to be fair.
🔄
Inner · Turning her back accusation
He says she's shutting down / turning her back when she ends the conversation
▼
If she chooses to respond at all:
"I'm not turning my back. I'm done with this conversation for now." Tap to copy
She is not turning her back. She is protecting what is left of her bandwidth. The conversation that has reached the end of its usefulness does not need to be extended because he is not satisfied with the ending. She ends it when it is over. That is the ISTP's right.
The Rules She Holds

She responds to what is named. Not what is sensed. The unnamed weather is not her responsibility until it has a name.

One sentence then done. She does not add to it, defend it, or revisit it.

She does not perform warmth she doesn't have. Civil is sufficient. Civil costs less.

The floor does not move. Two events per month. She holds it without explanation.

The private spaces are hers. The morning. The bathroom. The reading corner. Non-negotiable.

The exit is being built quietly. She does not share the build. She simply builds.

The goal is not connection. The goal is stability at minimum viable cost.
