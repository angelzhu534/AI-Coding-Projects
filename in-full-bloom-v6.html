Sun in 6th house: you are your rituals.

  • One candle — botanical, lit before work begins. Without exception.
  • Fresh flowers when possible. One stem from Warm Water Cove market Wednesday.
  • Natural light facing you. Dogpatch gets real morning sun — use it.
  • Headphones as boundary signal. In = unreachable.
  • Matcha setup within reach. Making it is the work-start transition.
  • Clean surface. Venus in 6th Virgo: clutter is a psychological tax.

On camera

Presence translates.

Light in front. Backdrop: bookshelf, neutral wall, or flowers visible at frame edge. Saturn ASC: you look composed on camera in a way people can't explain. Tattoos visible at collarbone are personality, not liability.

Day structure

Movement before work. Always.

Moon-Mars in Capricorn: run first. Deep work before noon. Calls in the afternoon. At 6pm: close all tabs, change, walk to Crane Cove. Capricorn moon needs a formal end-of-day signal.

The remote life principle
"Working from home is not the absence of office. It's the construction of a private temple. The candle, the flowers, the matcha, the clean surface — these aren't aesthetic preferences. They are the architecture that makes the work possible."
