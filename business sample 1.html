About This Summary
All figures below reflect the mature, steady-state business at current slider settings — not the ramp period. During the 6-month ramp, revenue starts at ~10% of these figures and builds to 100%. The model is structured as an LLC filing as an S-Corp: no entity-level federal income tax. Net income shown is pre-owner personal tax (owner pays individually on distributions).

What isn't in these numbers
WA State taxes: B&O (~$461/yr) and Public Utility Tax (~$2,958/yr) are not deducted here — subtract ~$3,419/yr from net income for a more conservative view.
Owner personal taxes: S-Corp distributions are taxed on the owner's personal return. The advisory estimate is ~$40–50K/year in personal tax obligation on distributions — this is not a business expense and doesn't appear in the model.
Ramp period: All figures assume full mature volume. In months 1–6, expect significantly lower revenue and negative monthly cash flow covered by the operating cushion.
Call mix risk: COGS shown is blended. A shift toward residential bariatric calls (which cost ~$268/call in COGS vs. $66 institutional) will compress margins.
